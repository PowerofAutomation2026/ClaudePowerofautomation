          How the stress score is calculated

          A fixed, transparent heuristic from 0 to 100 over the last 10 probe rounds of one service. It is not a prediction and not machine learning; it never estimates a time to failure.

          score = round(100 × min(1, 0.5×D + 0.2×J + F))

          D (drift): where the median of the last 3 answered probes sits between the learned baseline (0) and the warning threshold (1), clamped to 0..1.
          J (jitter): mean absolute change between consecutive answered probes, divided by the baseline, clamped to 0..1.
          F (failures): failed probes in the last 5 rounds × 0.5, capped at 1.
          Blocked probes are ignored. Until a baseline is learned, D and J are 0. Bands: 0-29 calm, 30-59 elevated, 60-100 high.

          outage-watch reports what its own probes saw. It does not read Microsoft's status page and cannot confirm a root cause. "Blocked" means your own proxy or firewall refused the probe, not an outage.
